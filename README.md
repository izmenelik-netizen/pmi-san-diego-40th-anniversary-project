# PMI San Diego 40th Anniversary Exhibit

## Project overview

This repository documents my contributions to PMI San Diego’s 40th Anniversary project. My primary responsibility is developing a historical timeline exhibit and supporting event materials for the anniversary celebration at the San Diego Zoo.

## My role

**Exhibit Design Coordinator**

My responsibilities include:

- Researching PMI San Diego and regional history
- Organizing content into historical periods
- Developing exhibit content and layouts
- Coordinating feedback and revisions with stakeholders
- Following PMI branding and event requirements
- Supporting additional event materials

## Project objectives

- Present PMI San Diego’s history from 1986 to 2026
- Create an accurate and engaging historical exhibit
- Produce materials for physical and online viewing
- Complete deliverables before the September 12, 2026 event
- Maintain consistent messaging and visual standards

## Key deliverables

- Historical timeline exhibit posters
- Online exhibit content
- Scavenger-hunt materials
- Event-program content
- Prize-voucher templates

- Research and fact-checking
